**Why finding 2 is the blocker.** `utils/testdata/small/src/test.proto:68` defines `LegacyBufValidate` with `(validate.rules).string.email = true`, `legacy_buf_validate.pconf` feeds it the invalid value `"hello.world"`, and `compiler/lib/compiler_test.go:54` asserts that compile fails with `ErrInvalidConfig`. That test exists for the sole purpose of proving PGV annotations are enforced. There is no v1.x mechanism to keep it passing.

**The cascade.** The `_ ".../legacy"` blank imports are not decoration. `go list -deps ./compiler/lib/parser` confirms they are what pull in `github.com/envoyproxy/protoc-gen-validate/validate`, which registers `validate/validate.proto` into `protoregistry.GlobalFiles`. That registration is what lets `test.proto:6`'s `import "validate/validate.proto"` resolve. Remove the blank imports without also removing that proto import and the entire `small` fixture stops parsing, breaking far more than one test.

**Not affected:** `buf/validate/validate.proto` stays registered transitively through `buf.build/go/protovalidate` itself, so the `BufValidate` message and `compiler_test.go:53` survive untouched under either option.
