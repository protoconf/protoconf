## Claude's Discretion — decided here

CONTEXT.md deferred one choice and asked for a decision plus a reason.

**`TestCompilerStartupScaling` STAYS in the existing `-race` `Run coverage` step.**
(RESEARCH.md's Open Question 2 recommended moving it; that recommendation is declined, on
the evidence below.)

1. **No correctness reason to move it.** Its gate is the allocation ratio, measured at 0.91x
   plain and 1.02x under `-race`. The race detector does not move the number it asserts on.
   The new non-race step exists for the one measurement that genuinely cannot survive
   `-race` — wall clock — and moving a race-insensitive test into it dilutes that single
   legible reason.
2. **The saving is noise.** The race detector costs it ~2.3s (0.98s -> 3.3s locally). The
   `build` job's observed baseline is ~11.5-12 minutes (runs `34183056828`, `34132541240`,
   `34198954421`). Under 0.5% of the job.
3. **Moving it costs bookkeeping that can rot silently.** It would need
   `-skip 'TestCompilerStartupScaling'` in `Run coverage` *and* a matching `-run` entry in
   the new step. Edit either side later and the test runs twice, or — the failure mode that
   matters — runs nowhere while both steps stay green.
4. **It keeps its Codecov contribution.** RESEARCH.md's Assumption A2 (that the coverage
   delta is negligible) was explicitly not measured. Leaving the test where it is makes A2
   moot instead of betting on it against a 1% project threshold.

Consequence: **the `Run coverage` step is not modified at all.** The budget test stays out
of it by skipping itself under `-race`, which also protects the pinned local command
`go test -race -timeout 300s -skip 'Test_cliCommand_Run' ./...` — that command would
otherwise run the wall-clock gate under the race detector and fail it.

**Step placement:** the new step goes immediately after `Build` and *before* `Run coverage`.
`Run coverage` is the long (~11.5 min) and historically flaky step; a budget step placed
after it does not run at all when coverage fails — which is exactly when the D-03
calibration observation is needed. Order is otherwise irrelevant: neither step consumes the
other's output.
